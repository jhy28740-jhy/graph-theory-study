Relation to the 𝑆2 theorem
The comparison with 𝑆2 is conceptual rather than a claim
that the proof methods are the same. Huang and Wei use
edge maximality, a threshold rule, chain-graph complements,
a weighted Ferrers spectral-tail inequality and exact rank-one
optimization to obtain a finite-order uniqueness theorem [13].
The 𝑆3 reductions developed here instead move from a rankthree projection to a common isotropic measure and then to
shared Toeplitz/Cauchy resources. The common theme is that
the successful proof must preserve the equality geometry while
reducing dimension; the mechanism by which this happens is
different.

Certificate layers
The proof archive contains four distinct classes of verifier.
(a) Symbolic identities: projection reductions, rank-six decompositions, common-carrier Cauchy identities, exact
determinant or congruence factorizations.
(b) Rational/interval positivity: Bernstein coefficients, shifted
LDL factors, outward interval-Krawczyk maps, and de
Casteljau subdivisions.
(c) Finite exhaustive graph checks: canonical generation plus
exact characteristic-polynomial/root comparisons for fixed
orders.
(d) Falsification tests: dense grids and nonlinear optimizers
used only to locate difficult branches or to reject overly
weak relaxations.
